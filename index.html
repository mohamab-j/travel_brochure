Our Story

Welcome to Stellaris

The World's First Orbital Luxury Resort

Founded in 2041 and orbiting at 400 km above the planet, Stellaris Space Resort is the pinnacle of human achievement and hospitality. Every detail — from our zero-gravity sleeping pods to our panoramic observation domes — has been engineered to give you an experience that is literally out of this world.

We complete a full orbit of Earth every 92 minutes, which means guests enjoy up to 16 breathtaking sunrises and sunsets every single day. Our crew of 120 specialists is dedicated to ensuring your comfort, safety, and wonder at every moment of your stay.
